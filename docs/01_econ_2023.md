## 6. The 2020 case sheet and the origin of "345 mbbl"

`Tec-12 (1)` is a values-only paste (no live formulas) from the September 2020 model: monthly TEC-12 cash flows for four WTI cases at USD 1.55 MM capex, and a summary table:

| Tec-12 #2 | 45 | 300 | 400,000 | 1,789,927 | 85 % | 16 | A32:G32 |
| Tec-12 #3 | 50 | 300 | 400,000 | 2,312,769 | 108 % | 14 | A33:G33 |

Row 21 holds the TEC-12 monthly rate profile with `J21` = 345,981 bbl. `J21` is a pasted number, not a formula; summing the row's own rates × days gives 400,254 bbl to the end of the sheet (Jul 2055), and 345,981 bbl is reached only in production month 293 (Nov 2045), which no economic case runs to. The case table's "400,000" is the full-sheet cumulative rounded; "345" is the cumulative to some earlier cut-off that is not recorded in the sheet. The `Summary (GLJ)` sheet then back-solves volumetrics to hit exactly this number (section 7). So the "345 mbbl type curve" quoted through the review and the deck is a cumulative of the 2020 profile at an unrecorded date, and the 2024 model's own economic TEC-12 recovery is 234,629 bbl incremental (355,678 bbl gross TEC-12 within the combined economic life). Logged as G-18.

## 8. Re-derivation and cross-checks (LibreOffice recalculation not possible, G-16)

LibreOffice 24.2 is installed but cannot load any file headlessly in this container (tested on xlsm, xlsx and txt, with and without a private profile), so the workbook was not recalculated. Instead every cached value was re-derived in pandas from the primitives:

| Identity checked | TEC-10 case max abs diff | Combined case max abs diff | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue = oil × field price (rows 386, 428, 454) | 0.00008 USD | 0.00008 USD within contract; 27,423 USD only in 2059 | pass |
| Royalties = basic + bid + surface + HC tax (471+478+480+482 = 483) | 600 USD (2029, post-limit) | 5,692 USD, 2059 only | pass |
| Opex = fixed + variable (493 + 498 = 500) | 12,600 USD (2029, post-limit) | 37,242 USD, 2059 only | pass |
| NOI = netback × flag + capital (577) | as above | as above | pass |
| NPV10 = Σ NOI × monthly discount factor (row 73) | 1,355,679.18 exact | 4,192,547.24 exact | pass |

The residuals sit entirely in months after the 35-year contract ends (Jan–Jun 2059), where row 77 zeroes revenue and opex while the primitives continue, and, in the TEC-10 case, in post-limit months where the fixed costs continue in the sheet but are flagged out. Within the economic period the cached values are arithmetically consistent, so the saved workbook can be relied on without recalculation.

Two column-J "sheet total" cells are not meaningful and must not be quoted: `J585` (depreciation total, −15,975,000) and `J586` (taxable income total, −2.33 × 10⁹). Row 586 carries losses forward cumulatively (`L586 = ... + IF(K586<0,K586,0)`), so its column sum is a sum of running balances; row 585's OFFSET declining-balance depreciation is likewise not meant to be summed. Tax (`J587`), NOI after tax (`J588`) and NPV after tax (`J590`) are sums of monthly cash amounts and are fine.

## 9. Flags for the review and the handover

1. **TEC-12 is modelled as a USD 1.8 MM horizontal re-entry of TEC-10, not the USD 1.568 MM S-shape new drill** in the AFE and Work Program (G-17). Either the model or the AFE must be restated before the two are compared.
2. **The TEC-12 profile is the September 2020 curve shifted to 2024**, unchanged by TEC-11 (2021) or by three further years of TEC-10 history (G-18). The 300 bbl/d IP is the same number the 2020 cases used.
3. **"345 mbbl" is a pasted cumulative of unrecorded cut-off**, and the `Summary (GLJ)` volumetrics are tuned to reproduce it (G-18).
4. **The model's own TEC-12 recovery is 235 kbbl incremental / 356 kbbl gross** within the economic life, not 345 kbbl; the 400 kbbl in the 2020 case table is the full-sheet profile to 2055 with no economic limit.
5. **The `Sensitivity (NEED TO UPDATE)` sheet is stale** (TEC-11-era capital of 124,100 and old prices) and was not used (G-19).
6. **Dates**: file name 2023-09-29, OUTPUT stamp 2023-10-24, Drive modified 2025-08-18 (G-03, G-20).
7. **No corporate G&A, no working-interest split, no Simmons carry** in this model. NPVs are 100 % working interest, before the 40/20 carry described in the review.
8. **API 40 is a deliberate fudge** (`C5` note) to avoid a gravity discount; the true 28–30° crude would be priced lower under the PEMEX formula. The 90 % of WTI factor may already embed this, but the workbook does not say so.
9. **Currency**: USD throughout, confirmed by row labels. CAD conversion is not attempted here per the kickoff rule.
