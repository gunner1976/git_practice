**5. Possible TEC-11 liability (new question, G-56).** The 2019 pool carried a TEC-11 completion of 1,401,250 and an exploration pool of 7,585,151. From 2020 the completion is 206,225, which equals the "Tantayuca incl. testing" line, and the pool is 6,459,000 (−1,126,151). The difference is about the 962,850 of "Workstrings invoices" in the TEC-11 cost summary. At end-2019, about 1.9 MM of TEC-11 capital was unpaid, and the 2020 forecast scheduled 1.97 MM of "accrued capital" payments. Removing the completion cost suggests the Workstrings invoices were disputed, written off or reclassified. **Ask whether they were settled, waived or are still owed.** This is a due-diligence item.

**6. Fiscal regime as paid** (`1b8XUWRP…`, all LOS). Contract royalty 31.22 %; base royalty `Bn × price + 1.5 %` (7.5 % floor, 20 % cap; 7.5-10.9 % in practice); landowner royalty 1 % of revenue net of royalties, paid annually.
