# Regulatory folder (TECOLUTLA DATAROOM / Regulatory) - findings

Folder id 1CnwGgN6DaT_yw5O6s384BEph6ggpfY0X. The folder holds the 2016 licence contract, the 2018 PEP crude-sale contract and its exhibits, and the CNH Evaluation Plan in three states:
- the December 2016 English original;
- the 2017-05-19 Spanish filing, with annexes A to J, tables, budgets, AFEs and the May-2017 replies to CNH;
- the January 2018 Spanish revision.

There are **no** CNH monthly production or measurement reports, ASEA, SENER or SEMARNAT permits, SAT filings, abandonment notices or fines here. No per-well 2020-2022 production and no TEC-7 injection volumes were found.

## Ranked findings

1. **Change of control needs CNH consent** (contract 16HcngJW, cl. 23.2). This applies to Kinjal buying 100 % of Tonalli.
   - Base term is 25 years (2016-08-25 to 2041-08-25), plus two conditional 5-year extensions. The repository's 35-year horizon (02_econ_lineage) assumes both extensions.
   - Guarantee of USD 1,764,100.
   - Abandonment trust under joint CNH control, funded quarterly as AA = max[0,(PAE/RR)·CAE − IA] (G-49).
   - The rendered text ends inside Anexo 3, so Anexo 6 (Programa Mínimo) is missing.

2. **A January 2018 amendment moved the workover from TEC-2 to TEC-7** (plan docx 1AVKfjDV, budget 11aXvxDN). This is new to the repository.
   - Only the well name changed. The workover stays at USD 676,372 and the plan total at USD 10,201,253.
   - It explains the 2019 TEC-7 workover (USD 95,848 actual), after which TEC-7 became the injector (G-57).
   - The filed TEC-7 programme (1idOhOyO) copies TEC-2's casing depths. Its KB of 4.80 m matches PEMEX (G-58).
   - Proof of CNH approval is not in the folder.

3. **The minimum work programme is 4,600 work units** (Tabla 1 1Hv6BHQ8, Tabla 9 1ZX9DeIa, Table 06 1AqkXHTp).
   - The plan offered 4,978 work units. TEC-10 drilling alone is 4,000 (87 %), with workover 800, seismic 143, core 25 and water analysis 10.
   - Whether CNH credited it, and the guarantee status, must be confirmed (G-49).

4. **CNH net oil is a prorated balance of the CAB Poza Rica fiscal meter** (Exhibit I 1alZyhyT, Exhibit A 1-Lqu9jZ).
   - Field tank gauging only sets the daily proration.
   - A Coriolis meter was required at Ezequiel Ordóñez.
   - This explains why the 2020 filing's 16,132 bbl net differs from the 18,973 bbl in tickets. G-53 is narrowed, not closed.

5. **The flaring basis is weak** (Anexo J 1Lj4Q41y, Gas Conservation 153qNw94/1mNdILjr, item E1 1x5qtZTW).
   - The Mexican norm is 98 % utilisation of associated gas. Tonalli declared 6,000 m³/d produced, of which 4,000 m³/d is flared.
   - All conservation options showed negative NPV10: −3.7 to −7.5 MUSD on capex of 5.0 to 8.5 MUSD.
   - That economics used a GOR of 60 m³/m³ (337 scf/bbl). Measured GOR in 2018-2021 was 742-859 scf/bbl, about 2.5 times higher (G-31). This is a regulatory exposure.

6. **TEC-10 under-performed every forecast filed with CNH** (Curvas de Producción 1ElAw2u1, Tec 7 oil forecast 1_4sj0VY).
   - Filed EUR was 430-691 kbbl from initial rates of 250-750 bbl/d. The 2018 forecast alone was 41.7 to 125.1 kbbl.
   - Actual was about 48 kbbl by March 2020, below the Low case (G-22, G-39).

7. **The offsets carry integrity liabilities.**
   - TEC-6 (1BnsQV8U, on the TEC-10/TEC-12 pad) has a casing break at 352 m and was plugged in 2007.
   - TEC-9 (1a4FKOi9) has a casing break from 544 to 718 m. It had a workover from 2012-10 to 2012-11 and was plugged on 2013-01-04, which corrects the repository's "closed 1999".

8. **TEC-2 KB is 3.80 m, with ground at 0.02 m**, in Tonalli's 2016 programmes (1YqpsXn3, 1aMys1fp) and the 1956 PEMEX report (1B3pJC5L). The repository uses 4.00 m (G-58, 0.2 m difference).

9. **Seismic: Earth Signal proposal** (11vg2ENd).
   - The survey is a "partial" 3D of 4,729 shot records, about 105 km². The repository gives about 150 km².
   - Deliverables include SEG-Y PSTM gathers, stacks and velocity volumes, for USD 78,000. Earth Signal is a direct source for G-55.

## Contradictions and QC flags

- The 2018 TEC-7 programmes carry TEC-2 casing data and the 460 m flowline length.
- The English TEC-2 programme header says TEC-10.
- The ERA (18207XAC) contains PEMEX template material (Dos Bocas injection at 15,000 bbl/d and a 127.9 MMscfd release case), and the PHAST release conditions are generic. Do not cite them.
- Tabla 3 gives a TEC-3 rig release before its spud.
- The core length is 10 m in the Spanish tables but about 18 m in the English ones.
- The PEP quality annex gives 28 °API alongside density 0.966, which are inconsistent.
