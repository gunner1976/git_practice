## Ranked findings

1. **GLJ has never lowered its TEC-12 type well, and the repository disagrees with it.** GLJ's EUR has been 400 kbbl P50 and 600 kbbl 3P since YE2019. The 1P EUR has been 250 kbbl since YE2020. Against the repository: the GLJ 1P EUR (250 kbbl) is above the repository P50 (218 kbbl). GLJ's 2P reserves (326–352 kbbl remaining, 400 EUR) are 1.5–1.8 times the repository base. The 343 kbbl figure the repository cites is the YE2020 export-workbook value; the published YE2020 to YE2022 2P figures are 335 to 352. For the presentation, expect "GLJ says 400, you say 218". The answer is the TEC-10 analogue. GLJ still carries TEC-10 at a 300 kbbl 2P EUR, yet TEC-10 had produced 77.1 kbbl by 2022-12-31 and was barely producing (3.5 bbl/d calendar average in 2022). This adds to G-39.
2. **The proved booking history is not monotonic, and YE2019 had no proved undeveloped.** At YE2018, 419 Mbbl was booked as PUD (first attributed in 2018). At YE2019, after TEC-11 tested water, GLJ cut 1P to 74 Mbbl, all TEC-10 PDP. TEC-12 and TEC-13 were horizontal wells booked as probable only. TEC-12 became proved at YE2020, as a directional well, "based on offsetting production and existing surface pad synergies" (YE2022 text). Kinjal may ask why proved status was restored without new data.
3. **No producing reserves since YE2022.** At YE2022 and YE2023 all developed reserves are non-producing. TEC-10 needs a paraffin workover (15 k$ planned, Feb 2023). TEC-2 was worked over in Dec 2022 and restarted at 23 bopd, but GLJ calls it uneconomic. Every proved undeveloped volume depends on TEC-12, whose date slipped from Jul 2021 (YE2019) to May 2021 / 2022 / Sep 2023 / 2025. This is consistent with the CNH shut-in from July 2022 (docs/13).
4. **There are two YE2023 reports, and they are not on the same basis.** The Tonalli report (Feb 2024) uses SPE-PRMS, is in USD and says the partners hold "Jaguar and IFR 50 % each". The IFR report (Apr 2024) uses NI 51-101 and public-domain production data. Its values are in CAD (base price 89.40 = 67.50 USD / 0.755), and its volumes are exactly 42.6 % of Tonalli's. That share matches IFR's diluted 42.63 % at 2023-12-31 (accounting_notes; briefing line 9). Do not quote the IFR YE2023 NPVs as USD.
5. **The field cumulative supports G-30 and G-11.** GLJ's well-level cumulatives give 1,490.1 Mbbl at 2019-10 and 1,533.1 Mbbl at 2022-12-31: TEC-6 507.2, TEC-9 352.6, TEC-2 329.2, TEC-7 267.0 and TEC-10 77.1 Mbbl. GLJ gives PEMEX-era production as 1,443 Mbbl. The By-Zone monthly records total 1,936,255 − 481,767 = 1,454,488 bbl. The 2.0 MMbbl field cumulative is therefore not supported by GLJ either.
6. **GOR conflict (G-31).** GLJ's twelve-month statistics to 2019-10 give a field GOR of 1,342 scf/stb (TEC-10 1,265; TEC-2 1,787). TEC-10 in 2022 shows 8,162 scf/stb. GLJ's YE2022 economics use 900 scf/bbl. The repository's measured values are 565–860 scf/bbl. This needs a reconciliation of the gas metering, or the gas numbers should be flagged as estimates.
7. **GLJ economic inputs, for comparison with docs/10.** Price was 86.9 % of Brent at YE2019 and 80 % at YE2022. Fixed opex was 1,670 $/well/month plus 227 k$/yr at YE2022, against 2,200 $/well/month plus 294 k$/yr at YE2019. Abandonment is 75 k$/well. TEC-12 capex was 2.4 MM$ as a horizontal well (2020 $) and 1.9 MM$ as a directional well (2023 $). That is consistent with the repository's 2.04 MM$ AFE. Full detail is in res_review.csv.
8. **The YE2021 final equals Draft 1.** This confirms the repository figures. Base-case 2P PV15 was 7.959 MM$, so the 9.134 MM$ used in the 25 Aug 2022 Jaguar valuation was a price sensitivity.
