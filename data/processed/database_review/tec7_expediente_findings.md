# TEC-7 expediente de perforación: findings

Source: `data/raw/colab_out/BLOQUE_TECOLUTLA_7_EXPEDIENTE_PERFORACION.PDF` (215 scanned pages, Oct 1956 to Oct 1998), read by eye. Per-page index: `tec7_expediente_pages.csv`. About 45% of the pages are duplicates. Nothing is dated after 6 Oct 1998. Depths are mbmr (below rotary table) unless marked mbnm. 1 m³ = 6.29 bbl; 1 kg/cm² = 14.22 psi.

## Ranked findings

1. **No log prints or curve images.** The only graphics are a tops-only panel (p76), maps (p77, p88), pressure plots (p49-53, p113) and schematics (p16, p34). What survives is log-derived: corrected tops (p70), Sw and porosity for 2310-2329 m (p84-85), dipmeter results (p87-91) and the list of logs run (p122, p124).
2. **The KB is 4.80 m, not 5.00 m.** Every 1956-1971 PEMEX document gives E.M.R. 4.80 m and ground 1.34 m (p16, p35, p48, p70, p96). The 6.00/1.5 m on p97 are marked "estimated".
3. **El Abra top: 2309 mbmr / -2304 mbnm, corrected from the electric log (p70, p72).** This confirms the repository's -2304.0. Cuttings record grey shale, grey sandstone and black chert at 2303.6 m. The first "caliza crema densa" (cream dense limestone) is at 2308.4 m, and the same limestone continues to 2315.9 m and 2340.0 m (p125-126, p123). No oil show is recorded in the cuttings. The cement job sheet says "no oil manifestation below the shoe" (p141). The E-log analysis puts the top of the limestone at 2310 m (p84).
4. **The initial rate depends on which document you read.** The 23 Jan 1957 two-hour test on a 6 mm choke flowed 89 m³/d (560 bbl/d) of oil and 8,300 m³/d of gas, GOR 93 m³/m³ (520 scf/bbl), FTP 68 and FCP 54 kg/cm² (p79). The radiogram (p78) gives the same rate with water 4.8% and a "capacity" of 180 m³/d. The informe final (p70) gives 89 m³/d, GOR 70 and water 2.2%. The repository's 352 bbl/d equals the 56 m³/d quoted in 1964 and 1971 (p56, p36), which is the first full day on the battery (25 Jan, p117).
5. **Shut-in history confirmed.** The open interval "was rapidly invaded by salt water" and the well was shut on 12 Mar 1957 (p56). The 1957 interval ended at 15 m³/d with 44% water on a 3 mm choke, salinity 50,000 ppm (p36). The 1964 repair program (a plug at 2330 m and new perforations at 2325-2327 m, p56-57) was approved (p54) but evidently not carried out: in Dec 1964 the well was still shut with perforations at 2335-2337 m (p48). The well reopened on 9 Oct 1971 (p23).
6. **The 1971 workover (9 Sep - 6 Oct 1971, rigless, p114-116 and p159-161):**
   - 2335-2337 m squeezed with 5,000 kg of Apasco cement plus kerosene and DOC-3, tested at 210 kg/cm²;
   - 2310.0-2313.0 m perforated (2305-2308 mbnm, p23), Scallop 1-9/16" guns, 39 shots, depth set by radioactive log;
   - acidised with 2 m³ of 15% HCl;
   - result on a 5 mm choke: 76 m³/d (478 bbl/d) oil, GOR 78 m³/m³, water 0.2% (p23, p115).
   The program had proposed 2324-2327 m (p37, p169). The interval actually shot was 11-14 m higher.
7. **No depletion over 34 years.** Static pressure was 251.90 kg/cm² (3,583 psi) at 2310 m on 12 Dec 1964, with water level 2045 m and oil to surface (p48). On 6 Oct 1998 it was 250.63 kg/cm² (3,565 psi) at the 2311.5 m mid-perforation. The 1998 wellbore held a brine-weight column of 0.103 kg/cm²/m, with no packer, 2-7/8" tubing at 2337 m and the bottom plug at 2338 m (p112). Both surveys fit a strong aquifer drive.
8. **Water encroachment after 1971 was erratic.** In 1975 water cut swung between 0.3% and 100% (p12-13). In 1979-80 it swung between 1.6% and 99% (p11). Typical 1980-84 rates were 4-6 m³/d oil on 2-4 mm chokes, with 80% water in Jan 1980 and 0% on 30 Sep 1984 (p2, p10). Not producing in May 1985 (p3).

## Logs, cores and tests

- **Logs.** 3 Jan 1957: E-log (sand sonde) and caliper, 2313-601 m; dipmeter at 4 stations, 2018-2310 m. 10 Jan 1957: E-log (limestone sonde) and microcaliper, 2333.7-2300 m; radioactive log, 2333.7-2000 m. 1971: radioactive log for perforation depth control.
- **E-log analysis (p85).** 2310-2317 m: Sw 0.13, P 0.17. 2317-2323 m: Sw 0.29, P 0.12. 2323-2329 m: Sw 0.18, P 0.13. P is probably porosity.
- **1971 reserve inputs (p169).** φ 0.13, Sw 0.20, Bo 1.391, RF 0.20. Proved reserve 5,652 m³ (35.6 kbbl).
- **Dipmeter.** Last station 2300-2310 m: dip 16°10', direction N87W, flagged as unreliable (p87, p89).
- **Cores and DSTs.** None. The informe final states "no se efectuó ninguna prueba de formación" (no formation test was run, p70).
- **Cumulative to Mar 1957.** 4,129.0 m³ oil (26.0 kbbl) and 664.5 m³ water (p56). The 1971 program gives a different figure, 3,471 m³ (p36).
- **Oil properties.** Density 0.863-0.880 g/cc (about 29-32 °API). Formation water salinity 37,900-39,100 ppm in 1957.

## Contradictions with the repository

1. KB is 5.00 m in the repository; PEMEX gives 4.80 m throughout.
2. Liner top is 2281.0 m in the repository; PEMEX gives 2281.2 m.
3. The repository lists plugs at 2325 m (permanent) and 2330 m (mechanical). Neither exists in this file up to 1998, which shows only the 2338 m cement plug and no packer. The 2330 m plug appears only as an unexecuted 1964 proposal. Both plugs must therefore date from after 1998 (probably the 2019 Tonalli work) or be mis-sourced. Check `Anexo 3`.
4. The repository's initial rate of 352 bbl/d is the 56 m³/d first battery day. The tested rate was 89 m³/d (560 bbl/d).
5. The repository says 2335-2337 m is "squeezed / below plug". It was cement-squeezed in 1971, and no plug was set.
