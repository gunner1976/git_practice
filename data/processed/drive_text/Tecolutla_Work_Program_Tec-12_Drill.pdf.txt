Summary of First Phase of Go-Forward Work Program
- Tonalli intends to commence with further development drilling at Tecolutla.
- The first development location is Tec-12 which is a low risk location directly offsetting the damaged Tec-9 well.
- There is un-perfed El Abra pay at the top of the Pemex Tec-9 well that was identified by Pemex as a completion opportunity. Also, un-perfed El Abra pay at the top of Tec-6.
- The casing near the top of Tec-9 was severely damaged by Pemex during milling in an attempt to enter the well to perf. This resulted in Pemex cementing the well.
- Tec-6 was abandoned before the upper section was perfed. We do not know the reason why Tec-6 was abandoned.
- Analogous El Abra pools suggest further upside on Tecolutla.

Merits of Tec-12 Drill: Offset to Tec-9 (Cum. Oil = 353k bbl)
1) Tec-12 is offsetting Tec 9 which was a good producer and the upper portion of the well was not perfed.
2) Tec 12 is interpreted to be in a structurally higher position to the perfs of Tec 10, Tec 6, Tec 9, Tec-7 and Tec-2.
3) Tec-12 will have very low op costs. Current op costs are mostly fixed. Incremental operating costs will be limited.
4) Tec-12 will be a directional drill from existing Tec 10 pad site. The Tec-10 pad site was built for multi-well development.
5) Tec-12 will have a very low execution risk because of past Tec-10 & Tec-11 drills.
6) Tec-12 will come on-stream quickly. There is a gathering line connection at the Tec-10 pad site.
7) Tec-12 has good offset laterally and vertically from other wells and perfs.

Tec-12 Well Design and Preliminary Cost Estimate
- Planned TD @ ~2352 TVD (2370 mMD). Directional plan needs to be finalized. Tec-12 will have a similar design to Tec-9. WBM 1.2-1.25 g/cc (or Invert)?
- Total Cost: $1,500,000
- Reach TD with 8 1/2" bit; ideal for logging and allows contingency plans; 5" liner as a contingency plan in the event of hole problems. Tec-2, Tec-6, Tec-7 included a liner, but TEC-9 did not. Decision not to run liner in Tec-9 was likely based on the structural integrity of the Tantoyuca and El Abra observed in the previous wells. Tec-9 didn't have drilling issues other than a human error by dropping a piece of equipment that modified its initial directional plan. Tec-12 is proposed to build a shallow and smooth angle towards NE and either finish it with an angle like Tec-10 or get back to vertical, like Tec-9. Directional Plan is currently being discussed.
- Tec-12: Preliminary Drill and Complete Cost Estimate — rough pricing based on 2018-2019 pricing.
- TEC-12 Drill & Complete Target: 14 days. Hammer 13-3/8" conductor as in TEC-11. Finish 12 1/4" section in 4 days, 9 5/8" casing cemented. Reach TD (2350 MD) with 8 1/2" bit and 7" casing cemented in 7 more days. Fast 3 day completion before releasing the rig (perf guns on wireline, tubing and packer). Be more aggressive on bidding.
- TEC-11 Depth-Time Curve vs Tec 12 plan: pump failure on surface hole; building to 61 degrees, autodriller not functioning; last 24 hours only drilled 25 m from 2332 to 2357 MD at ~61 degrees; issues running liner (tie-back string faulty and damaged, fishing, two cement jobs); lost time running intermediate (hydraulic arm failure); from CP 2357 to finished cementing 51.5 hours.
