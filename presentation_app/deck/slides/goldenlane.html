The Golden Lane (Faja de Oro)

An Albian–Cenomanian reef that ringed the Tuxpan carbonate platform: an atoll about 150 km long and 70 km wide. Its rim is a chain of separate fields. Cerro Azul No. 4 (1916) blew out at ~260,000 bbl/d, uncontrolled for 9 days, and made 57+ MMbbl.

Where Tecolutla fits

On the onshore rim at its south-eastern end, about 60 km from Poza Rica and 7 km from the coast. CNH play: El Abra · reef rim · onshore. Licensed in Ronda 1.3 (2016). The adjacent TM-01 block went in Ronda 2.3 (2018) to Jaguar E&P on a USD 26.1 MM tie-break bonus.
