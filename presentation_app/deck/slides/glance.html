How big is it? Small: 2.5–3.1 km², OOIP 7.8–11.2 MMbbl, about 2 MMbbl produced. Remaining recoverable 0.3–1.6 MMbbl, most likely about 0.9 MMbbl at the trend recovery factor. Official baseline: PEMEX/CNH booked 1P 0.0 / 2P 0.0 / 3P 0.1 MMbbl at 1 Jan 2015. IFR’s 2018 pre-drill OOIP of 26 MMbbl may surface; it predates TEC-10 and TEC-11.
