What if TEC-9’s sonic porosity is overstated by shale? With a 20 % shale correction the window’s Sw is 1.1–1.3 times the producer’s; with 40 %, 1.5–1.6 times. That is why the programme measures clay directly. Anti-collision and shallow integrity: TEC-6 on the same pad was abandoned in 2007 with casing problems and poor cement from 45 to ~2,000 m (G-63). Field GOR stayed 400–1,100 scf/bbl through decades of crestal production, so any remaining gas cap is small.
