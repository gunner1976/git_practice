The story in one line

A small reef with a strong aquifer, produced from the flanks upward. Its highest El Abra, on the crest, has never been perforated.

TEC-12 is designed to log it, test it and complete it, with a proven fallback interval below.

This is the thread through the deck: geology and drive explain why oil remains at the top of the crest; the production history shows every well moved upward with the water; the 2018–19 programme proved the column between the old producers; TEC-12 is the well that reaches the one part of the field never produced.
