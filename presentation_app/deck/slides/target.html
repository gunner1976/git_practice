“Your own logs at TEC-10 say the upper zone is wet. Why drill for it?” At TEC-10 those depths are entirely Tantoyuca on the cuttings XRD (El Abra from about −2,310 on logs, −2,313 on cuttings), so they say nothing about El Abra at the crest; TEC-101 shows the same low-resistivity signature above its El Abra top at −2,334. TEC-9’s top 8 m is porous and at parity with its producer; TEC-7’s top unit was oil-bearing in 1957. TEC-7 produced its top interval 1971–2006 and tested water in 2019, so current saturation at the crest is what TEC-12 measures. Restated target: the top ~8 m of El Abra, reached 14–20 m higher than at TEC-10.
