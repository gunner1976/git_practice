TEC-12: the well

An appraisal-producer, not a gamble

Trajectory

S-shape from the TEC-10 pad, 175 m east-north-east. El Abra at −2,296 mSS (2,323 mMD); TD 2,360 mMD (−2,333 mSS); 7 in casing to TD; about 13 days.

Data before completion

Density–neutron–PE, resistivity and image log across 2,290–2,335 mSS; formation pressures; a bottom-hole fluid sample, the field’s first PVT.

Cost and forecast

AFE USD 2.04 MM (Nov 2020 estimate indexed on the US drilling PPI). Base 180 bbl/d, EUR 218 kbbl on TEC-10’s decline; GLJ 1P/2P/3P EUR 250/399/578 kbbl.

It turns the hardest question about the field, what is at the top of the crest, into the well’s stated objective, with a producing fallback either way.

Why not re-perforate TEC-6 or TEC-9 instead? TEC-6 was abandoned in 2007 (plugs, casing problems, poor cement) and TEC-9 was plugged in 2013 with casing breaches; neither has modern logs. A new well gives logs, a sample and a clean completion at the crest.
