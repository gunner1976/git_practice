The idea: a long lateral at the top of the oil

Vertical wells cone

Every vertical here drew the bottom water into a cone round its perforations and watered out, often within months.

A horizontal spreads the drawdown

720 m of open hole at the top of the column makes the same rate at a fraction of the drawdown, so water arrives later.

The Jan 2019 plan carried three horizontals at 694 bbl/d each, against TEC-10’s 149.

That concept has never been tested.

Concept from the Jan 2019 operator deck (docs/18 A5); horizontal productivity screening Babu–Odeh. Schematic, not to scale
