The PEMEX PSTM puts TEC-12 on the crest

PEMEX PSTM: time high on TEC-6, TEC-12, TEC-9, TEC-10. TEC-12 is earlier than 97 % of the field; time tracks the tops (r = +0.69).

Depth at TEC-12: −2,293 m (±~4 m) from the crestal wells’ velocities: 3 m above TEC-9, 17 m above TEC-10.

DMI PSDM: sharper, but images the top of the carbonate package, not El Abra. The grid’s northern high is not real on the PSTM.

Which seismic do you trust? For the El Abra top, the legacy PEMEX PSTM: it ties the wells and needs only a smooth well-velocity field (2,914–2,951 m/s; leave-one-out error ±2.5–2.7 m at the crestal wells TEC-6, TEC-9, TEC-10, 13–18 m at flank wells). The 2018 DMI PSDM’s reef reflectors are highest in the west, opposite to the well tops (r = −0.48): log tops sit at the reflector at TEC-9/TEC-6, ~20 m below at TEC-10, ~45 m below at TEC-101, because a Tantoyuca marl overlies El Abra and thickens off the crest. Dominant wavelength ~67 m, so the seismic cannot tell whether El Abra at TEC-12 starts at the top of the package (like TEC-9) or ~20 m lower (like TEC-10): that is the well’s test. Still missing: Earth Signal PSTM, DMI gathers, angle stacks and velocity model.
