What the marl is

At the crest the window is reef; off the crest it is marl

Tantoyuca marl: Eocene shale and silty marl that buried the eroded reef. TEC-10 cuttings: 31 % quartz, 9 % clay, 50 % calcite, pyrite. El Abra is 91–100 % calcite.

Why it matters: TEC-10’s “wet” upper zone is marl, not reservoir. At the crest the same depths are reef, and that is what TEC-12 goes to measure.
