Production history

Sixty-six years, five producers, one aquifer

1956–71. Salt water shuts TEC-2 and TEC-7 within months; TEC-6 carries the field alone.

1971–2012. Recompletions higher up give the 932 bbl/d peak (Mar 1972); TEC-9 adds 353 kbbl. Water, not pressure, ends each well.

2018–23. TEC-10 makes ~77 kbbl on solvent clean-ups; TEC-2 restored. Last oil January 2023.

Why the gaps in the record? They are reported zero-production months: the original CNH tables carry 781 explicit zero rows that fill all 66 gap runs; an intermediate copy had dropped them. Field cumulative 1.98 MMbbl to end-2022 (1.72 in the monthly record plus PEMEX wellfile allocations). Tonalli-era per-well volumes after Nov 2019 are commingled (~90 % TEC-10 by the 2021 CNH filing). GLJ says the wells were shut in early 2023 for an environmental permit; the CNH filings give July 2022; counsel to reconcile (G-65).
