TEC-12 from the surface

One pad, a short step-out to the crest

Drilled from the existing TEC-10 pad, about 120 m from the old TEC-6 well: no new lease, road or flowline; shares the battery and the TEC-7 disposal well.

S-shaped deviated well: kicks off and steps out about 175 m east-north-east to the crest, finishing between TEC-6, TEC-9 and TEC-10.

TEC-6 is ~120 m from the TEC-10 pad (casing parted at 352 m), so anti-collision is in the programme.
