Colour: blue deep flank (−2,420 m) to red highest (−2,280 m); 10 m contours.

NW–SE elongated high at the south-east end of the Faja de Oro El Abra rim, faulted on both flanks. Tertiary Tantoyuca rests directly on the reef.

Top El Abra, mSS: TEC-6 2,294 · TEC-9 2,296 · TEC-2 2,302 · TEC-7 2,304 · TEC-10 2,310 · TEC-101 2,334.

The 2019 depth grid honours every vertical well within ±2 m. The dashed −2,374 m lowest-known-oil contour closes round the field.

Where is the oil-water contact? Not one flat number. Original free-water level 2,380 mTVDss (2019 operator deck); PEMEX 1956 oil–salt-water about −2,373 and GLJ lowest known oil −2,374.2, both from the TEC-3 drill-stem test (980 m of 24 °API oil plus 80 m of salt water); a possible current free-water level about −2,316 (2019 deck); wet TEC-2 perforations at 2,331–2,345 mSS. Production history shows coning around producers rather than a flat rising contact. Flank wells TEC-3, TEC-5 and TEC-101 found El Abra low and wet; they define the edges.
