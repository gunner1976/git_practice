TEC-10DES, 2018

Marl above, oil-bearing El Abra below

Brown, 2,294–2,311 mSS: Tantoyuca marl. Low resistivity (~5 ohm·m), high gamma, quartz and clay on cuttings.

Blue, below 2,311: El Abra reef. Resistivity 16–47 ohm·m; the orange band is the producing perforation.

Perforated 2,314–2,318 mSS just below the reef top; 181 bbl/d first full month, k 18 mD.

Message: the “wet” upper zone is marl, not reservoir. TEC-10 never sampled the crestal El Abra that TEC-12 targets.

Why only 3.5 m perforated? Tonalli perforated the high-resistivity interval directly below the El Abra top; the low-resistivity zone above is the Tantoyuca, not reservoir.
