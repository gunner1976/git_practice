Your own capture

The built-in levels are procedural Gaussian clouds. For a photoreal body, load a real 3D Gaussian Splatting capture of a person: a .splat file or a 3DGS .ply (point_cloud.ply). It becomes the top rung of the ladder and shares every render control.

Where a photoreal body scan comes from

A splat is trained from photographs, so photorealism needs a capture of a real person. Phone apps that export Gaussian splats: Polycam, Luma AI, Scaniverse, KIRI Engine (export as .ply or .splat). On a desktop, Postshot or Nerfstudio train from a video walk-around. Keep exports under about 1 M splats for smooth orbiting.

Scan settings

Scans are sorted per frame on the CPU. Above ~1 M splats, lower the resolution setting if the frame rate drops.
